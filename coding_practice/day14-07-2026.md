Key topics covered so far:

1.Kubernetes Architecture & Setup: Practical demonstrations on connecting to an AWS EKS cluster using kubectl and managing credentials.

2.Namespace Management: Explanation of Namespaces as logical boundaries within a cluster , including default namespaces like default, kube-system, and kube-public.

3.Networking & Load Balancing: Detailed comparison of Application Load Balancers (ALB) and Network Load Balancers (NLB), explaining how they operate at Layer 7 vs. Layer 4 of the OSI model.

4.Workload Controllers: In-depth look at ReplicaSets for scaling, StatefulSets for persistent storage needs , and DaemonSets for node-specific tasks.
